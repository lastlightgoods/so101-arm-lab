  • Three cameras is the limit the Jetson records cleanly at 30 fps. Add ② now; add ④ only for the plug work, and drop ① if the loop rate falls.
  • Mounts must not move between recording and running a policy. Mark their positions on the table with tape.
  • Keep ② on the side opposite you, so your hand and body stay out of its frame during demos.
  • The object zone is 229 × 200 mm, inside the reach arc and away from the far corners where the arm fails.
