## Candidate notes

### 1. Transcend Small Nibbler v1.0 ($149, assembled)
Product page: "Parallel long stroke gripper for the SO-101 arm... Made to order in polymer, and ships with everything needed to mount and run it." Nothing else is specified there (https://transcendmechanics.com/arms/so-101); the catalog adds 2 fingers, 144 mm stroke (https://transcendmechanics.com/grippers). Terms: USD, US shipping with cost at checkout, Delaware corp in Milford CT, 30-day unopened returns, 1-year warranty (https://transcendmechanics.com/legal/terms, /legal/warranty). The vendor's own plugin repo describes "FastGripper — a worm-gear-driven, compliant parallel gripper for the SO-101": the servo is "a stock Feetech STS3215 kept in multi-turn feedback mode" on bus ID 6, "Motor layout is identical to the stock SO-101", stroke "~4.8 servo turns", self-locking worm, full stroke "4–6 s", run as `--robot.type=fastgripper_follower` with `pip install lerobot_robot_fastgripper`; the plugin imports `lerobot.robots.so_follower`, which exists in v0.6.1 (https://github.com/Transcend-Mechanics/fastgripper-lerobot; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/huggingface/lerobot/v0.6.1/src/lerobot/robots/so_follower/so_follower.py has `"gripper": Motor(6, "sts3215", MotorNormMode.RANGE_0_100)`). The gripper still reads as `gripper.pos` 0–100, but calibration becomes the plugin's setup/park routine. The product page never says the Small Nibbler is the FastGripper; it is the only SO-101 gripper they sell, so treat that as an inference. Photos show a housing replacing the whole stock gripper at the wrist, flat textured pads with two screw holes per tip (removable "stock-metal" tips, customer tips "on request", per https://github.com/Transcend-Mechanics/fastgripper-mujoco), and no camera boss.

### 2. Robonine parallel gripper (recommended print-at-home build)
- STLs (models/parts): Main frame x1, Clamp (D6.0/6.1/6.2/6.3 variants, pick by rod fit test plate) x2, Gear rack .031 x2, Gear x1, Nail x2, plus Camera holder, Camera spacer, Holder (no-camera) and D405 holder; a combined "Follower gripper (165x165 bed size).STL" prints everything in one plate.
- Print settings per repo: "Material PLA / PETG", 0.2 mm layers (0.15 for gears), 20% infill (30% for gears, README table says 100% for gear and racks), 3–4 walls, supports as needed, "light sanding may be needed for bearing fits". README: "8 parts (~100-150g PLA)", "Print the Parts (2-4 hours)", assembly mass 170 g at 30% infill. (computed) solid volumes: frame 30.5 cm³, clamp 13.8 x2, rack 1.5 x2, gear 1.3, nail 0.13 x2, camera holder 16.8, spacer 0.7: about 80 cm³, ~99 g PLA if solid. No CF filament is called for; PETG is the stated upgrade, and the hardened nozzle makes PETG-CF possible for the frame if you want stiffness, though the repo does not recommend it.
- Non-printed hardware (BOM page prices): 2x MF106ZZ 6x10x3 bearings "~$9.99 (10 pcs)"; 2x D6x125 mm rods, cheapest listed "Carbon fiber tube 6x4x200 mm (2 pcs) ~$5.29" (cut to 125 mm); 2x M4x8 DIN7991 "~$6.99 (100 pcs)"; 4x M2x8 DIN912 "~$6.79 (120 pcs)"; 4x M2 nuts "~$5.99 (100 pcs)"; 4x M3x4 set screws "~$6.99 (100 pcs)"; servo 1x ST-3215-C018 "~$29.00" (reuse the arm's); servo horn/disc and its M3x6 and self-tapping screws come "from the servo kit". Full-pack hardware ~$42 without servo; BOM states "~$50" pro-rated with servo (https://github.com/roboninecom/SO-ARM100-101-102-Parallel-Gripper/blob/main/docs/bom.md).
- Mounting and LeRobot: holder bolts "to the wrist joint horn of the SO-ARM101 with four M3x6 screws", cable to servo 5, "Configure a unique servo ID" (keep 6), so stock `so101_follower`, calibration and 0–100 range stay as they are (https://github.com/roboninecom/SO-ARM100-101-102-Parallel-Gripper/blob/main/docs/assembly-guide.md). Camera: spacer + "UVC camera with 28x28 mm mounting holes", 4x M2x8; the spacer footprint is 38x38 mm (computed).

### 3. PincOpen (Pollen)
Printed parts total 152 g PLA plus a 5.6 g "Interface_ARM100" part (BOM sheet masses); hardware is 4x 3x16 and 4x 3x24 h6 dowel pins, 20x M2.5x8 thread-forming screws, 4x 3x6x5 brass bushings, 4x M3x10, 4x M3 brass inserts, about 2.75 EUR total; the sheet's 26.46 EUR includes an STS3215 and a bus board you already own. Material column says PLA throughout; the grip-tip doc recommends rigid PLA/PETG tips with an adhesive anti-slip layer ("3M GM400") or TPU tips at 30–50% infill (https://github.com/pollen-robotics/PincOpen/blob/main/docs/grip_tip.md). Stroke is not stated and the interface is for SO-ARM100, not explicitly SO-101.

### 4. Grip4SO101 and ggao50
Grip4SO101: PLA, parts split into 15% and 100% infill plates ((computed) 216 cm³ + 20 cm³, so roughly 300 g of PLA at those infills is plausible but not stated); hardware 5x 12x18x4 and 4x 3x10x4 bearings, 6 mm GT2 belt, screw kit, 2x MGN7C 150 mm rails with carriages; prices not stated, and the Amazon/AliExpress pages returned 503/login redirects. ggao50: 6 printed parts, (computed) 139 cm³ total, 105 mm opening, no BOM, servo and screws unspecified, assembly only in Onshape.

### 5. Seeed flexible gripper
Not parallel ("deformable contact structure", "No reliable numerical ratings are published"); will not centre a nut. Servo not included so bus ID is unaffected. No Seeed-hosted page found.

## Recommendation
With a P2S on the way, print the Robonine gripper: it is the only print-only design with a stated stroke, force, print time, filament, infill table and a priced BOM; it reuses the arm's STS3215 as ID 6 with stock LeRobot 0.6.1 and 0–100 calibration; and it has a documented UVC camera holder, so the icSpring moves to the holder (hand-eye must be re-solved for any new gripper anyway). Print the frame and clamps in PETG, gears and racks at 100% infill, and order bearings, rods and fasteners (~$42 retail packs). For the 10 mm nut, the clamps' flat PLA/PETG faces will centre it across flats; add a thin adhesive anti-slip layer to each clamp face, as Pollen recommends for PincOpen tips, instead of relying on bare plastic. If you would rather not build, the Small Nibbler is the only assembled, US-shipped SO-101 parallel gripper found, with flat removable pads and a self-locking hold, at the cost of $149 plus shipping, an unstated lead time, a vendor plugin and robot type, and no camera mount; email info@transcendmechanics.com first to confirm lead time, included servo, stroke and camera options. Use a print service only if the printer is delayed.
